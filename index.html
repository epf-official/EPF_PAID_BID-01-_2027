TOURNAMENT RULES & REGULATIONS

Official rules and guidelines for EPF PAID BID TOURNAMENT - 1.

1. Roster & Squad Rules

  • Each team must register a minimum of 6 and maximum of 8 players.
  • Player 1 must be designated as the Team Captain.
  • On each Match Day, Captains must submit a 5v5 starting squad before match start.
  • Player IDs and device names must match the registered profile exactly.

2. Match Format & Matchups

  • Tournament consists of 12 Match Days in a Double Round-Robin format.
  • Each Match Day features 5 individual 1v1 player matchups between two teams.
  • Matchups are randomly generated once both team squads are approved by Admin.
  • The team winning the majority of 1v1 matchups wins the Match Day (3 Points).

3. Super Match & Tie-Breaker

  • If a Match Day score ends in a draw (e.g., 2-2 with a draw matchup), a Super Match is triggered.
  • Each Captain selects 1 representative player for the Super Match.
  • The winner of the Super Match claims the 3 points for that Match Day.

4. Points & Grand Final

  • Match Day Win = 3 Points, Loss = 0 Points.
  • Goal Difference (GD) serves as the primary tie-breaker on the Points Table.
  • Top 2 teams from the Points Table after 12 Match Days advance to the Grand Final.
